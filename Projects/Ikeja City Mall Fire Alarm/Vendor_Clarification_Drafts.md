# IKEJA CITY MALL — FIRE DETECTION & ALARM SYSTEM OVERHAUL
## Vendor Clarification Requests — DRAFTS FOR REVIEW

**Status:** Draft content only. Not formatted as formal letters. For client review and approval before conversion into issued documents.

**Prepared:** 1 August 2026

---

### How these drafts are built

Each of the five drafts below is self-contained and follows the same structure:

| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| **Section A** | General clarifications. Identical wording in all five drafts. |
| **Section B** | Vendor-specific clarifications, as directed by the Client. |
| **Section C** | Additional points identified during the evaluation. Flagged separately so the Client can include or drop each one before the letters are issued. |

**Three notes before reviewing:**

1. **No competitor pricing is disclosed anywhere in these drafts.** Where a vendor's price is challenged, the challenge is anchored either to independently verified market pricing or to that vendor's own figure expressed as a normalised rate (per metre of cable route, per device). No vendor is told what another vendor quoted.

2. **Vendor names** are written here as the Client refers to them. The names appearing on the submissions themselves are TotaGO Technologies, Emo-Technical Services Ltd, Potreal Insight Solutions Ltd, JML / Fadayomi, and Roundbox Solutions Services. The exact legal entity name should be confirmed for each before the formal letters are addressed.

3. **Item 13 percentage.** The drafts require the fee to be expressed as a percentage of Items 1–12 supported by a build-up, but they do not state a target percentage. Naming a figure invites every vendor to price at it. If the Client prefers to state an acceptable band, it can be inserted at A7 in all five drafts.

# DRAFT 1 — TOTAGO

**To:** TotaGO Technologies
**Subject:** Request for Clarification and Revised Submission — Proposed Overhaul of the Fire Detection and Alarm System, Ikeja City Mall
**Your reference:** Quotation dated 20 March 2026

---

Further to our review of your submission against the Request for Proposal issued for the above project, we require clarification on a number of technical and commercial matters before the evaluation can be concluded.

Your submission remains under consideration. This request is issued to every participating vendor and should not be read as an adverse finding. It is, however, a condition of continued evaluation that a complete response is received by the date stated at the end of this request.

Your response should consist of a revised quotation, a technical assessment report, and written answers to the specific points raised in Section B.

## SECTION A — GENERAL REQUIREMENTS (ALL VENDORS)

**A1. Revised quotation for the entire project**

Please re-submit a revised quotation covering the entire scope of the project. The revised quotation supersedes your original submission in full and will be the document evaluated.

**A2. Complete coverage of the bill of quantities**

Every item listed in the Request for Proposal must be quoted, with complete technical details. Where you consider an item unnecessary, it must still be addressed in writing, with your engineering reasons stated, rather than omitted without comment.

**A3. Product identification for every quoted item**

For each item quoted, state:

- the exact product model;
- the product code;
- the manufacturer reference or order number;
- the full technical specification;
- the specific variant being supplied;
- any further product identification that unambiguously defines the exact item proposed.

A quotation that describes a category of device rather than a specific part number cannot be evaluated against the other submissions received and will be treated as incomplete.

**A4. Technical assessment report**

Alongside the revised quotation and invoice, submit a technical report based on the inspection you have already carried out at the mall. This is not additional work: the Request for Proposal already requires the contractor to assess the existing system and identify defective components.

**A5. Content of the technical assessment report**

The report should address, as a minimum:

- the condition of the existing fire alarm system;
- the viability of the existing system;
- the extent of damage to the existing installation;
- the reasons for recommending the proposed overhaul;
- the engineering justification for the solution you propose;
- where you propose any variation from the specification in the Request for Proposal, the reasons for that variation;
- where you quote a particular variant of an item, the reasons that specific variant was selected.

**A6. Specification and variation of all equipment**

For all equipment quoted, state clearly the exact specification and variation being supplied. Where a product family contains variants that differ in performance or price, identify which variant is offered and confirm the rate quoted corresponds to that variant.

**A7. Item 10 — Expansion Panel**

Every vendor is required to address this item. State whether the additional expansion panel is technically required, having regard to the fact that the main 1–4 loop panel proposed appears capable of accommodating the estimated number of devices currently installed within the mall.

Your answer should be supported by engineering reasoning rather than asserted. Where you maintain the expansion panel is required, identify the constraint that drives it — loop loading by device type, current draw on visual alarm devices, loop length, zoning, network architecture, or the physical distribution of the building — and support it with a loop loading calculation.

**A8. Item 13 — Professional Services**

Item 13 is to be revised. Rather than a lump sum, present the professional service fee as a reasonable percentage of the total value of Items 1 to 12, and state the percentage applied. Support it with a build-up showing the man-days, rates, commissioning duration and documentation effort on which the figure rests.

**A9. Manufacturer certification**

Where you propose to retain the existing Gent system, confirm in writing whether you hold the qualifications and certifications required to program, commission and support a Gent fire alarm system. Name the certified commissioning engineer who would be assigned to this project and provide the certificate reference.

## SECTION B — CLARIFICATIONS SPECIFIC TO YOUR SUBMISSION

**B1. Item 1 — Main control panel**

- Specify the exact panel proposed, by model designation and order code.
- Explain the panel variation offered, including loop capacity, the number of loop cards fitted as supplied, and any optional features included in the rate.
- The sum of NGN 7,000,000 quoted against this item stands materially above independently verified market pricing for a panel of this description, which sits in the region of NGN 4.5m to NGN 4.9m. Please review this rate and either substantiate the premium by reference to what the rate includes, or revise it.

**B2. Items 2, 3, 4 and the Gent S4 addressable sounders**

Specify the exact variations and product codes intended for supply against each of these four lines. Your submission identifies these items by device category only, and they cannot be evaluated on that basis.

In particular, Items 2 and 3 are each quoted at NGN 71,000 per unit. Item 3 is specified as a heat detector carrying an integrated visual alarm device, which is a materially more expensive product than a plain detector. Confirm which variant of Item 3 is being supplied and whether the rate quoted corresponds to that variant.

**B3. Item 8 — Piping, trunking and containment**

Explain in detail what is included within the piping, trunking and containment scope: the containment types and sizes, the route lengths, the fixings and supports, and the accessories allowed for.

**B4. Item 10 — Expansion panel**

Explain why the additional expansion panel is necessary, having regard to the capacity of the main panel you propose. See A7 above for the basis on which this should be answered.

**B5. Item 12 — Civil and builders' works**

- Explain the civil works scope in detail, itemising what the allowance covers.
- The sum of NGN 3,500,000 quoted equates to approximately NGN 6,480 per device installed. This appears excessive for the works described and should be reviewed and reduced where appropriate.

**B6. Item 13 — Professional services**

The sum of NGN 15,000,000 quoted equates to approximately 20.9% of Items 1 to 12, or some NGN 27,800 per device for installation, testing, commissioning, documentation and training. Revise this into a reasonable percentage of the total contract value for Items 1 to 12, supported by the build-up required at A8.

## SECTION C — ADDITIONAL POINTS IDENTIFIED DURING EVALUATION
*(For Client decision — include or omit before issue)*

**C1. Arithmetic on Item 9.** Four loop cards are shown at a stated unit rate of NGN 300,000, which multiplies to NGN 1,200,000. The line total reads NGN 1,500,000, and the subtotal printed on your quotation balances only with the higher figure. Please correct this in the revised quotation. Two further rates on the face of the document are also mistyped ("3,430,000,00" at Item 4 and "3,000,00.00" at Item 10) and should be corrected.

**C2. Loop card configuration.** Item 9 calls for four identical loop cards. A panel of the type specified is supplied with one loop card already fitted, and a card for the expansion panel is a different part number from a card for the main panel. State how many cards are fitted as supplied in the panel you offer, how many additional cards are required for the main panel, and the separate part number for any card belonging to the expansion panel.

**C3. Item 11 — Network cards.** Networking requires a card in each panel. Two panels are specified. Confirm how many network cards your price includes and whether the panels as quoted can in fact be networked.

**C4. Item 6 — Interface modules.** The specification states a channel count but not whether the modules are inputs, outputs or combined. Provide an input and output schedule identifying what each module will monitor or control, and confirm the product code of the module offered.

**C5. Validity.** Your quotation stated a validity of seven days from 20 March 2026 and has long lapsed. The revised quotation should carry a validity period of not less than 90 days from the date of submission.

**C6. Commercial particulars.** Your submission did not state delivery period, project execution timeline, warranty period or payment terms, each of which the Request for Proposal requires. These are to be provided with the revised quotation. Warranty should be stated per line item rather than as a single blanket figure.

**C7. Provenance.** Confirm in writing that all goods offered are new, unused and obtained through an authorised supply channel, and state the country of manufacture for each item.

## CLOSING

Your response is required by **[insert date]**. Responses should be submitted to **[insert recipient and address]**.

Where any point above cannot be answered within that period, notify us in writing before the deadline stating which point is affected and when the answer will follow. A submission that does not address every point raised may be set aside.
