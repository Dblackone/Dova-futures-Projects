## NOTES

**Fields to complete per vendor:** contact name, their quotation date, vendor name in the attachment title, and the attachment itself. Everything else is common.

**Validity.** Ninety days is suggested. Three of the five submissions had already lapsed at the date of review, so a stated validity on the revised quotation matters. Adjust the figure to suit the intended award timetable.

**Deadline.** Use the same date for all five. A vendor who learns another had longer has a legitimate complaint.

**On the fairness paragraph.** It is there to keep all five engaged and to avoid signalling any ranking. It is accurate as written — every vendor is receiving a request, and none has been excluded at this stage.

**Round Box.** The paragraph applies to them as it stands. Their document asks for a price on the tendered Gent scope alongside their alternative, so "your submission remains under consideration" is true, provided the Client is genuinely prepared to consider a compliant re-quote from them. If not, that sentence should be dropped from their copy only.
